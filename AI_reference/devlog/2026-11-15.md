**What the bench runs and the reviews found**
- Speech-to-text heard the name alone badly (no context). With a hint and the "hey" prefix, 102 of
  108 synthetic samples are recognised, up from 65 (`research-notes.md`).
- Wake answers offered the time or date unasked; fixed (2 of 9 down to 0 of 27 in the author's
  bench).
- Bots page review: the voice bot would have appeared as a second "main assistant" card, and
  discarding a recording that was waiting would have cancelled the running reply. Both were review
  findings on the branch; the merge followed the review.
- App shell review: opening the window delayed closing the microphone, and a DevTools port could
  open into the window if an environment variable was set. Both were review findings; the hardening
  branch followed. Whether the dead proxy works as a second network layer was unverified at the
  review; later reported to hold on its own (`research-notes.md`).
- Spoken-text review: a long web address could still be read aloud when a forced cut landed inside
  it; `fix/spoken-urls` followed.
- No review started anything visible (no display, offscreen only).
