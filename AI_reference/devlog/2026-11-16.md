## False wakes and the window that exits (`fix/wake-false-accepts`)

- False wakes: long stretches of other people's speech (5.4-7.5 s), decoded with the wake phrase as a
  hint, got the phrase written at the start and woke the assistant at any confidence (0.26-0.46). A wake
  with a command now needs evidence: below 0.35 it is refused; below 0.55 on a segment of 4 s or more it
  is decoded again without the hint (opening words only) and must still have a word of the phrase, or
  one of its spellings, among its first two words.
- The decoder's own probability for the name is recorded (`phrase_p`) but not judged: a pronunciation
  the decoder doesn't know scores it 0.05-0.3 although it was said.
- **The bench's real wakes are shorter than the owner's.** The first synthetic run had 2.6-3.8 s
  segments, so none reached the recheck; the owner's own "phrase, command" runs about 3-5.5 s and often
  scores under 0.55, so the recheck decides many of his real wakes. The bench now has longer commands,
  but its numbers on rechecked real wakes don't carry over to his accent: only a run in the room does.
- The window: the wake-open called the app window a failure 20 ms in (systemd had not created the unit
  yet), so the Chrome window opened. Fixed; the shell now writes why it ended, and no longer segfaults
  after a clean quit.
- **Not verified:** a run in the room with the owner's voice, and on his display.
